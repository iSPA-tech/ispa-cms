# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part in iSPA CMS, in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient, welcome newcomers, accept constructive criticism, and focus on what is best for the
people who use and build iSPA CMS. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **hello@ispa.tech**. Reports are read by the maintainers and handled privately.
